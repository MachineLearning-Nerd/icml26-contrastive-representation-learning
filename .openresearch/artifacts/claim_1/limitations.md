# Claim 1 limitations and deviations

- This is a proof reproduction, not a full-scale training experiment.
- The universal conclusion is carried by the structured analytic certificate;
  the finite exhaustive audit is only an implementation check.
- The paper omits theorem-level support and finiteness assumptions that its
  density-ratio proof uses. The verifier makes them explicit.
- No claim is made about minimizers inside a restricted neural-network class;
  the paper quantifies over all measurable scorers.
