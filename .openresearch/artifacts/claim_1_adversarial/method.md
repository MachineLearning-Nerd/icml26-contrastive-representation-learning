# Adversarial method

The checker exhausts positive rational distributions on supports of size 2–4,
including likelihood-ratio ties, and compares the ratio ordering against every
permutation by exact arithmetic. It separately analyzes three support-mismatch
patterns for risk attainment.

Three controls deliberately make the statement false:

1. negative temperature reverses the likelihood-ratio ordering;
2. zero temperature makes the objective undefined;
3. a constant-only scorer family cannot reach the unrestricted AUC optimum.

Each control must be rejected as an assumption or quantifier violation rather
than mislabeled as a paper falsification.
