# Limitations and deviations

This route reproduces the numbers encoded in the authors' full-scale figures,
not the training. The extracted points have no released uncertainty. The three
critical points are exactly `m=0.4n`; a standard power fit with a free
coefficient has exponent 1.0, while forcing coefficient 1 yields about 0.943.
Therefore the displayed `n^0.94` label is parameterization-sensitive.

Verdict from this route alone: **BLOCKED** for independent verification.
