# Exact falsification target

Section 5 states that performance improves as `m` increases and saturates after
a critical size, and that the empirical critical trend lies between `sqrt(n)`
and `n`, closer to `n`. The setup uses shared negatives; Theorem 4.6 additionally
requires those negatives to be independent of positive pairs.

The released source does not identify the actual subsets, so independence
cannot be audited.
