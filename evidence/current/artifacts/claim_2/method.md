# Method

The checker independently reconstructs the final algebraic implication from the paper's KL/Pinsker/ranking/Jensen proof chain. It also enumerates 4,672 finite positive-law, negative-law, and candidate-law triples over supports of size 2–4. The enumeration is a stress test, not the universal proof.

The negative certificate changes the coefficient from 2 to 1 and must exit nonzero.
